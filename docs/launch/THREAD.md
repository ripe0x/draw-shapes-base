# Announcement thread (draft)

1/ Draw Shapes is live on Base: pull for prizes that hold real ETH. Every prize is a Shape or Draw Pack you
can redeem for exactly its ETH, any time.

2/ A pull picks one prize from a public board with Chainlink VRF. Cheaper prizes are more likely (odds ∝ 1 /
notional). The price is the board's harmonic mean, about 0.0151 ETH on the reference board, plus a 1 %
settlement fee and a per-batch VRF fee.

3/ You see the full price and every row's odds before you pull. If the price moves past your limit before
settlement, you're refunded. Pull 1–20 at a time.

4/ On average a pull returns about 85 % of its cost in ETH. The rest pays the Shapes creator's mint fee for
replacements, the game premium, keepers and the GRAIL fund.

5/ GRAILs are 0.5–5 ETH Shapes listed at 80 % of backing. A GRAIL exists only once fully funded, by 3 % of
every pull and by ENGINE trading fees. Its odds rise slowly while it waits to be won.

6/ ENGINE: 35M fixed supply. 25M is shared over 14 daily epochs among delivered pulls (85 % by count, 15 %
by a random bonus). Unearned days are burned.

7/ The ETH/ENGINE pool charges 2 %: 0.25 % operator, 1.75 % treasury, which funds GRAILs and liquidity.
After genesis, realised game surplus buys and burns ENGINE in small slices.

8/ ENGINE is speculative and has no claim on game ETH. Shapes on Base are a separate collection from Shapes
on Ethereum.

9/ Built on FWA's pull engine by Token Works, with Shapes and Draw Packs by ripe0x, Chainlink VRF and
Uniswap v4. Contracts are verified on Basescan: [link]. Audit: [link].
