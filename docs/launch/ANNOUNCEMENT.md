# Public launch announcement (draft)

**Draw Shapes is live on Base.**

Pull for ETH-backed prizes: every prize is a Shape or Draw Pack holding real ETH, redeemable at any time.

- **Selection.** Chainlink VRF picks each prize from a public board. Cheaper prizes are more likely, and the
  odds and price are shown before you pull.
- **Price.** A pull costs about 0.0151 ETH on the reference board, plus a 1 % settlement fee and one
  Chainlink fee per batch.
- **GRAILs.** These are 0.5–5 ETH Shapes listed below their backing. They appear only when fully funded, by
  3 % of every pull and by ENGINE trading fees.
- **ENGINE.** A fixed 35M supply. 25M is shared over a 14-day genesis among delivered pulls; after genesis,
  game surplus buys and burns ENGINE.

Shapes on Base are a separate collection from Shapes on Ethereum.

The pull engine is adapted from FWA by Token Works.
